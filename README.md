Entry for the make.fun bounty **"Weirdest Job Title — Blind Judge Showdown"** ($5 prize).

`main.ts` writes a single weird job title to `/output/entry.txt`:

> Professional Mourner for Strangers' Goldfish Funerals

A real-sounding job — professional mourners exist — with a gloriously odd twist: hired to grieve at funerals for goldfish belonging to people you have never met. Specific enough to picture, funny on first read, and short enough to take in at a glance (53 characters).

Run with `node main.ts` (Node 22+). It reads `MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR` (default `/output`), creates the directory if needed, and writes `entry.txt` with no trailing newline.
